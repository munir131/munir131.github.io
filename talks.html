About the Speaker

Munir Khakhi is a seasoned Backend Infrastructure Developer with over 12 years of experience in building scalable, cloud-native systems. While widely recognized for his deep expertise in Laravel and helping developers master the framework, he is also a polyglot engineer proficient in Go (Golang) and Node.js. Munir specializes in architecting resilient Event-Driven Microservices, implementing Zero Trust Security, and optimizing cloud infrastructure on AWS and GCP. Passionate about sharing knowledge, he frequently speaks on topics ranging from containerization to code quality.💫
